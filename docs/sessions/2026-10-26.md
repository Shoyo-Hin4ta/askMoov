## Changes

- `plan.ts`: lookups the run already resolved are remembered by `lookupKey(type, text, filters)`; a repeated lookup gets the same entity. - `prompts.ts` rule 3: grade, section_id and teacher_id are lookup-only; a period that names a section is a section lookup; any other period is the spec's `filters.period`; a student's numbers in one of their sections are entity student plus `filters.period` (a student has one section per period: 12,601 pairs in the seed, none with two).
