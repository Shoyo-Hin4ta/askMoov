## Changes

- `plan.ts`: lookups the run already resolved are remembered by `lookupKey(type, text, filters)`; a repeated lookup gets the same entity. The parse-error corrective continues the failed conversation (tool rounds plus the rejected reply), like the validation corrective; `LlmParseError` carries `transcript` and `reply`, filled in `llm.ts`.
- `prompts.ts` rule 3: grade, section_id and teacher_id are lookup-only; a period that names a section is a section lookup; any other period is the spec's `filters.period`; a student's numbers in one of their sections are entity student plus `filters.period` (a student has one section per period: 12,601 pairs in the seed, none with two). A first wording also called `period` lookup-only; the review caught it before commit and `prompts.test.ts` now pins the rule.
- `lookup-entity.ts` `chosenFor`: a person chip answers a lookup whose words (honorific aside) are all words of its name, so "Simpson" after a Simpson tie takes the clicked chip instead of tying again; "Lisa" and "Bart Reyes" never take Bart Simpson's.
- Connections: the checkpointer runs on the shared app pool; runtime pools set `idleTimeoutMillis` 5000, `maxLifetimeSeconds` 60, an idle-client `error` listener, and `attachDatabasePool` (`@vercel/functions` 3.9.11, pinned; 3.10.3 was four days old). Its source shows it only acts on Vercel and keeps the instance awake past the idle timeout after a release; pg reports a plain `pool.query` release under the request that opened the connection, so it is best effort, and the transaction pooler is the real fix.
- Tests: graph replay of run 9a207dea (fails on the old `plan.ts` with the same clarification, passes now), the surname chip graph test (fails on the old `chosenFor`), `lookupKey`, the parse error's transcript, rule 3, and golden case `teacher-bart-p4-classes-to-90` (32, measured on the seed as the teacher: 4 of 8 attended; passes live, run `ffe1a713`).
